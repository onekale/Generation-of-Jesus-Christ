Two Genealogies of Jesus

Matthew and Luke record distinct lines. This site preserves both.

Why are there two genealogies?

Matthew 1:1–17 traces the royal line through David's son Solomon, following the kings of Judah to Joseph, Jesus' legal/earthly father. Luke 3:23–38 traces a different line through David's son Nathan, and is often understood as Mary's lineage (or a levirate/adoptive line) — though interpreters differ. Both are preserved here as distinct branches, not merged into one sequence.

Matthew 1:1–17

Abraham → Isaac → Jacob → Judah → Perez → … → Jesse → David → Solomon → … → the Babylonian exile → … → Joseph, husband of Mary.

Luke 3:23–38

Jesus → Joseph (as supposed) → … → David → Nathan → … → Abraham → … → Adam → God. Preserved in its own sequence.
